# Game Design Document: [Project Title Placeholder]

## 1. Introduction
   - Briefly describe the game: An Idle MMORPG.
   - Genre: Idle, MMORPG.
   - Target Audience: Players who enjoy MMORPGs but have limited active playtime.

## 2. Core Gameplay Loop
   - **Idle Progression:** Characters automatically perform actions (e.g., combat, resource gathering) even when the player is offline.
   - **Active Management:** Players log in to manage their characters, upgrade equipment, assign tasks, and make strategic decisions.
   - **Long-term Goals:** Focus on continuous improvement, unlocking new content, and achieving significant milestones.

## 3. Character Progression
   - **Leveling System:** Characters gain experience points (XP) from idle activities and active quest completion. Levels unlock new skills, areas, and features.
   - **Skills:**
      - Active Skills: Powerful abilities players can trigger during active play sessions.
      - Passive Skills: Enhancements that boost idle efficiency or character stats.
      - Skill Trees: Allow for specialization and diverse character builds.
   - **Equipment:**
      - Tiers and Rarities: Weapons, armor, and accessories with varying levels of power and unique attributes.
      - Crafting and Upgrading: Players can gather resources (idly or actively) to craft and improve their gear.
   - **Stats:** Core attributes (e.g., Strength, Agility, Intellect) that influence combat effectiveness and skill performance.

## 4. Questing System
   - **Main Story Quests:** Narrative-driven quests that guide players through the game world and lore.
   - **Side Quests:** Optional tasks offering additional rewards and character development.
   - **Daily/Weekly Quests:** Repeatable quests encouraging regular engagement.
   - **Idle Quests:** Objectives that can be completed over time through idle activities.
   - **Rewards:** XP, currency, equipment, crafting materials, and reputation.

## 5. Social Features
   - **Player Interaction:**
      - Chat System: Global, party, and private messaging.
      - Player Profiles: Viewable profiles showcasing achievements and character setups.
   - **Parties:** Players can form temporary groups for tackling challenging content or gaining bonus idle rewards.
   - **Guilds (Clans):**
      - Guild Membership: Persistent player communities.
      - Guild Perks: Bonuses for guild members (e.g., increased XP, shared resources).
      - Guild Activities: Cooperative events or guild vs. guild competitions.
   - **Trading:**
      - Player-to-Player Trading: Secure system for exchanging items and currency.
      - Auction House/Marketplace: Centralized hub for buying and selling goods.

## 6. World and Exploration
   - **Persistent World:** The game world continues to exist and evolve, even when players are offline.
   - **Zones:** Different regions with unique environments, monsters, and resources. Unlocked through character progression.
   - **Events:** Dynamic world events that players can participate in for limited-time rewards.

## 7. Monetization (Optional - Brainstorming)
   - **Cosmetic Items:** Character skins, visual effects for skills, pets that don't affect gameplay balance.
   - **Convenience Items:** Boosts for idle progression speed (e.g., temporary XP boosts), inventory expansions.
   - **Battle Passes/Seasonal Content:** Offer a track of rewards for completing daily/weekly challenges.
   - **Ethical Considerations:** Avoid pay-to-win mechanics; focus on fair and optional purchases.

## 8. Future Plans
   - New classes or character specializations.
   - Expanded storyline and new zones.
   - Advanced guild features (e.g., guild housing, large-scale raids).
   - PvP Arenas or open-world PvP zones.
   - More complex crafting and profession systems.
